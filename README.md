Azure Clicker Engine
Welcome to Azure Clicker Engine v1.2, a fast-paced incremental clicker game built for those who love to watch their numbers climb.

🚀 Game Overview
Azure Clicker Engine is an idle-clicker game where the objective is to optimize your clicking power, collect rare gems, and forge diamonds to skyrocket your Total Net Worth.

Core Mechanics
Cookie Production: Click the main icon to generate Cookies.

Upgrades: Spend your hard-earned Cookies to level up your clicking efficiency.

Gem Hunting: Every click has a chance to find a Gem. Gather enough to forge powerful Diamonds.

Net Worth Tracking: Watch your wealth grow in real-time as your Net Worth Multiplier increases.

📋 Features
Intuitive UI: A clean, focused interface designed for maximum clicking comfort.

Scaling Economy: Upgrade costs and rewards are balanced to provide a satisfying progression curve.

Resource Depth: Manage two distinct currencies (Cookies and Gems) to unlock ultimate rewards.

🛠 How to Play
Click: Start by clicking the central cookie to accumulate your first batch of cookies.

Invest: Once you reach the target threshold (e.g., 50 Cookies), purchase an upgrade to improve your yield.

Forge: Keep an eye on your Gem count. Once you hit the required amount, Forge a Diamond to boost your Net Worth.

Optimize: Strategize your clicks to maximize your Net Worth Multiplier.
